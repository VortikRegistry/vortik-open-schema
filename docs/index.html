Hegotá

FOCIL has a different upgrade assignment.

EIP-7805 is Scheduled for Inclusion in Hegotá under EIP-8081. It is absent from Glamsterdam’s scheduled set. Hegotá activation dates are unset.

Context matters

Related concepts are not interchangeable.

Block-Level Access Lists (EIP-7928) are scheduled for Glamsterdam. BAL Sidecars (EIP-8146) are declined for Hegotá. Each proposal keeps its own state.
